# Choose a small verified collection and explicit rule semantics

Six fixed original puzzles replace procedural runtime generation: authoring and two independent solvers can establish uniqueness for the exact shipped layouts. Fixed clues and versioned puzzle IDs form a durable save contract. Limit subtraction and division to two-cell cages; omit hidden cages and linked boards because the available creator material does not specify their full semantics. Manual notes do not constrain hints, avoiding deductions from potentially incorrect player annotations.
